ClinicBot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

ClinicBot is a software service intended to help participating clinics manage appointment enquiries and bookings through WhatsApp and related application interfaces. This policy explains how information may be handled when a person interacts with a clinic using ClinicBot.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

The clinic you contact is responsible for its patient and appointment information. ClinicBot provides the technology used by that clinic to receive and manage enquiries. For questions about a particular appointment or how a clinic uses your information, contact that clinic directly. For questions about the ClinicBot service or this policy, contact [ADD YOUR PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on how a participating clinic uses the service, ClinicBot may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp account identifiers made available through the WhatsApp service.
  • Messages you send to the clinic, and replies sent by the clinic through the service.
  • Appointment details such as your name, requested date and time, appointment status, and the clinic or provider you wish to visit.
  • Technical and operational information needed to receive, process, secure, and troubleshoot requests.

Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, government identification numbers, or sensitive medical information through the chatbot unless the clinic has specifically provided a suitable and secure process for doing so.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to appointment enquiries and help create, update, or cancel bookings.
  • Allow clinic staff to manage appointment requests and communicate with you.
  • Maintain the service, prevent misuse, investigate errors, and protect its security.
  • Meet legal obligations and resolve disputes where applicable.

4. WhatsApp and service providers

Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled by Meta Platforms and its WhatsApp services under their own terms and privacy policies. ClinicBot uses WhatsApp Cloud API to exchange messages. Information may also be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers used to operate the service. Those providers should only receive information needed to provide their services and are subject to the applicable contractual and legal safeguards.

5. Sharing

Information is made available to the clinic you are contacting and to personnel who need it to handle your request. It may also be disclosed to service providers supporting the service, or where disclosure is required by law, necessary to protect rights and safety, or authorised by you. ClinicBot does not sell personal information.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, support the clinic's appointment-management needs, meet legal requirements, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may therefore vary by clinic. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect information; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. For appointment and patient records, contact the clinic that collected the information. You can also stop messaging the clinic through WhatsApp, although the clinic may retain records it is legally required or otherwise permitted to keep.

8. Children's information

ClinicBot is not designed to collect information directly from children without the involvement or authorisation of a parent, guardian, or the clinic as appropriate. If you believe a child's information has been submitted improperly, contact the clinic or the privacy contact above.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes or legal requirements evolve. The latest version will be published at this URL with its effective date.
