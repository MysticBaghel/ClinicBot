ClinicBot Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 31 October 2026

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your interactions with a clinic using ClinicBot.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact the clinic you communicated with and ask it to delete the information associated with your appointment or WhatsApp phone number.
  2. For a request relating to the ClinicBot service itself, email [ADD YOUR PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject line ClinicBot data deletion request.
  3. Include the WhatsApp phone number used to contact the clinic, the clinic name, and a brief description of the information you want deleted. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Verification and processing

The clinic or service operator may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. We aim to acknowledge requests within a reasonable time and process valid requests within the period required by applicable law.

Information that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate recordkeeping, security, fraud prevention, or the establishment or defence of legal claims. If information cannot be fully deleted, access to it will be restricted where appropriate and it will be retained only for the applicable purpose and period.

WhatsApp information

Deleting information held by a clinic or ClinicBot does not necessarily delete messages or information retained by WhatsApp or Meta. You may need to manage information held by those services separately through their own settings and privacy processes.
